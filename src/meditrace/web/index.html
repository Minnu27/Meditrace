01

Immutable source

02

Location-aware facts

Facts require a page and may include line ranges, exact quotes, and bounding boxes.

03

Explicit uncertainty

Every extracted fact carries confidence, including OCR certainty. Unsupported claims do not become answers.

04

Tamper-evident

Facts and answers are hash-sealed, logged in a hash chain, and anchored as signed Merkle roots. Only hashes are ever anchored.
